Lucas

A private assistant that helps its owner manage their own Google Calendar.

What it does

Lucas is a personal assistant run by one person for their own use. When the owner connects their Google account, Lucas can read the events in the calendars they authorise and, when asked, add or change appointments. It uses this to answer questions such as "what is on tomorrow?", to plan the day alongside the owner's task list, and to put new appointments in the right calendar.

Who can use it

Lucas is not a public service. It is not offered to anyone else, there is no sign-up, and it is used only with the owner's own Google account.

Your data

How Lucas handles calendar information, who processes it and how to revoke access are set out in the privacy policy.
